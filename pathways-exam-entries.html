Pathways & Exam Entries
A quick reference for the routes from Key Stage 3 into IGCSE, Further Pure Mathematics and International A Level. Use the exam codes when checking entries or preparing departmental paperwork.

1 · Maths pathways

At a glance

Students move through one of three Key Stage 3 routes before progressing to IGCSE Higher. The accelerated route reaches IGCSE earlier and adds Further Pure Mathematics; post-16 routes continue into IAL Mathematics or IAL Further Mathematics.
