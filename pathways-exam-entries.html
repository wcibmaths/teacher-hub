Pathways & Exam Entries
A teacher reference showing which students normally follow each qualification, the exact Pearson Edexcel entry codes and the usual November or June route.

A · Who sits what?

All KS4 pathways

IGCSE Mathematics A · Higher

All students normally sit Pearson Edexcel IGCSE Mathematics A Higher. Support and normal pathway classes complete the course through Years 10 and 11; accelerated students reach the qualification earlier.

KS3 Support→ IGCSE Mathematics A
KS3 Normal→ IGCSE Mathematics A
Accelerated pathway · Ma1

IGCSE Further Pure Mathematics

The accelerated pathway begins IGCSE content in Year 9. Year 10 and Year 11 Ma1 normally follow Further Pure Mathematics alongside IGCSE Mathematics A.

KS3 Accelerated→ Y9 IGCSE Higher→ Y10–11 Ma1 · Further Pure
Sixth Form Mathematics

IAL Mathematics

Students taking Mathematics in Years 12 and 13 follow the Pearson Edexcel International A Level Mathematics modular route.

IGCSE Mathematics A→ IAL Mathematics
Sixth Form Further Mathematics

IAL Further Mathematics

Students taking Further Mathematics in Years 12 and 13 follow the wider Pearson Edexcel modular route, including Pure, Further Pure, Statistics, Mechanics and Decision units.

IGCSE + Further Pure→ IAL Further Mathematics
Placement note

Accelerated placement uses assessment evidence and teacher input. Placement is re-evaluated mid-year where appropriate.

C · Exam timing and entry route

November
Accelerated IGCSE entry

Accelerated students normally sit IGCSE Mathematics A in the November series. Preparation typically includes a first mock in late May or early June, a main mock in late August or early September, and final revision through September and October.

June
Standard IGCSE entry

Support and normal pathway students normally sit IGCSE Mathematics A in the June series. Preparation typically includes a mock in late February or early March and final revision and past-paper practice through April and May.

IGCSE Mathematics A Higher

Available in the November and June series. Both Higher papers must be entered in the same series.

IGCSE Further Pure Mathematics

Normally taken by the accelerated pathway alongside Mathematics A. Both papers must be entered in the same series.
