Vision & Aims
The Senior School Mathematics Department aspires to nurture confident, curious and capable mathematical thinkers who embody the values of kindness, courage, respect, integrity and responsibility. We aim to create a dynamic and inclusive learning environment where every student is supported to achieve academic excellence and personal growth through mathematics.

Our vision

1

Excellence and innovation

We deliver a rigorous, internationally benchmarked curriculum through Pearson Edexcel specifications, building deep conceptual understanding and transferable skills. Adaptive digital tools, timely assessment and curriculum differentiation meet the range of needs in our classrooms. High achievers are challenged through accelerated pathways and enrichment, while others receive personalised support to make meaningful progress.

2

Collaboration and global citizenship

We build a culture of collaboration and international-mindedness through maths teams, competitions and peer-led initiatives. Events such as the UK Mathematics Trust challenges develop resilience, strategic thinking and teamwork, and promote intercultural awareness.

3

Holistic and lifelong learning

Mathematics is taught as a rigorous discipline and a foundation for creative problem solving. Lessons emphasise reasoning, real-world application and interdisciplinary connections. We encourage students to embrace challenge, learn from feedback and persevere.

4

Inclusive and supportive practice

Every student should feel known, supported and able to succeed. Teaching is informed by assessment data, guided by evidence-based strategies and responsive to individual need. Careful differentiation and consistent use of student voice give all learners equitable access to the curriculum.

5

Integrity and accountability

Academic integrity and personal responsibility are core values. Students are encouraged to take ownership of their learning, reflect on progress, and approach their studies with honesty and diligence. We aim to develop skilled mathematicians and principled learners.

6

Our commitment

Through an inclusive and aspirational programme we equip students with the mathematical fluency, critical thinking and ethical foundation they need to succeed in higher education and contribute meaningfully to the world.

Our aims

Promote academic excellence

Deliver a rigorous, well-sequenced curriculum rooted in internationally recognised frameworks. Teaching fosters deep conceptual understanding, procedural fluency and intellectual curiosity. Through adaptive planning and high expectations, students are challenged to think critically and reason logically.

Encourage holistic development

Mathematics is taught as a creative and dynamic discipline. We promote problem solving, abstract thinking and real-world application through interdisciplinary projects and inquiry-based learning. Students develop resilience, independence and confidence as they engage with increasingly complex challenges.

Foster a collaborative and inclusive community

The department values mutual respect, diversity of thought and a shared pursuit of excellence. Through the Mathematics Squad and participation in national and international competitions, students experience collaboration, leadership and community engagement.

Support individual growth

Every student is supported to reach their potential through personalised instruction, timely feedback and data-informed intervention. We foster a growth mindset, encouraging students to embrace challenge, learn from mistakes and take ownership of their learning. Individual and collective achievements are recognised and celebrated.

Model core values and academic integrity

Teaching is underpinned by kindness, courage, respect, integrity and responsibility. We promote ethical behaviour, academic honesty and reflective learning, guiding students to approach their studies with discipline, curiosity and self-awareness.

Prepare for future pathways

We prepare students for higher education, global careers and civic life by equipping them with mathematical fluency, analytical reasoning and a commitment to lifelong learning. The curriculum is designed to develop skills that extend well beyond the classroom.
