# Senior School Behaviour and Sanctions 2026/27

Source: Senior School Behaviour Logging Process and Behaviour Levels guidance.

## 1. Senior School Behaviour Logging Process

### One-off low-level behaviour

1. Log as a College Concern on iSAMS.
2. Speak to the student.

### Repeated low-level behaviour

Examples include:
- In your subject
- Across multiple subjects
- Lateness
- Organisation

#### If the repeated behaviour is in your subject

1. Discuss the proposed Keep In with HoD/HoF.
2. Record on iSAMS as Level 3, Lunch Time Keep In.
3. HoY confirms the Keep In date and time by email.
4. Once confirmed, the staff member giving the Keep In informs the student.
5. The staff member giving the Keep In emails the parents, copying in the Tutor and HoY.

#### If the repeated behaviour is across multiple subjects, lateness or organisation

1. Tutor can put the student on a Report Card.
2. Tutor informs the student.
3. Tutor emails parents, copying in HoY.

## 2. Higher-level behaviour concerns

### Lunch Time Keep In

For a one-off high-level behaviour concern where a Lunch Time Keep In is appropriate:

1. Discuss the proposed Keep In with HoD/HoF.
2. Record on iSAMS as Level 3, Lunch Time Keep In.
3. HoY confirms the Keep In date and time by email.
4. Once confirmed, the staff member giving the Keep In informs the student.
5. The staff member giving the Keep In emails the parents, copying in the Tutor and HoY.

### After School Keep In

For a one-off high-level behaviour concern where an After School Keep In is appropriate:

1. Inform the HoY of the incident.
2. HoY investigates and speaks with the student.
3. HoY logs the incident on iSAMS.
4. HoY informs parents.
5. HoY issues the Keep In.

## 3. Logging on iSAMS

Use Reward & Conduct Manager.

- Select the appropriate category.
- Give a description of what happened.
- The notification goes to the Tutor, HoY, AHP and DHP.

# Behaviour Levels and Sanctions

## College Concern, Level 1

### Example Behaviour

- Poor or incomplete work / Prep
- Failure to follow instructions / poor behaviour
- Misuse of device
- Incorrect uniform
- Unkindness
- Late

### Example Consequence

Verbal discussion with staff member.

### Communication Home

None.

### Who

All staff.

---

## Repeated Behaviours, Level 2

### Example Behaviour

Repeated College Concerns, for example:
- lateness
- uniform

### Example Consequence

Report Card.

### Communication Home

Email to parents.

### Who

House Tutor, with sign off from HoY.

---

## Level 3

### Example Behaviour

- 3x repeated instances of not handing in Prep for the same subject
- 3x late to Tutor in a week
- 3x wearing PE kit to lessons
- Repeated lateness to lessons
- One-off instances of higher-level poor / unsafe behaviour
- Multiple instances of rudeness / failure to follow instructions
- Aggression towards others / physical contact, e.g. pushing

### Example Consequence

Lunchtime Keep In, 30 minutes.

### Communication Home

Email to parents from the teacher giving the Keep In.

Parents invited in for a meeting as required.

### Who

Staff member giving the Keep In, with sign off from HoY.

---

## Level 4

### Example Behaviour

- Repeated Level 3 sanctions
- Cheating in internal tests
- Not handing in phone
- Truancy of lessons
- Repeated unkindness or high-level, one-off instances of poor behaviour
- Aggression towards others / physical contact, e.g. pushing / hitting

### Example Consequence

After School Keep In, 1 hour.

### Communication Home

Email to parents.

Parents invited in for a meeting as required.

### Who

Head of Year / Assistant Head.

---

## Level 5

### Example Behaviour

- Repeated Level 4 sanctions
- Cheating in internal exams, e.g. Mock Exams
- Bullying-type behaviour
- Serious physical contact, e.g. hitting

### Example Consequence

Internal Rustication, exclusion.

### Communication Home

Parent meeting to include Deputy Head.

### Who

Deputy Head / Head.

---

## Level 6

### Example Behaviour

- Multiple instances of Level 4-5 sanctions
- Bullying
- Serious physical contact, e.g. hitting
- Cheating in external exams
- Possession or use of prohibited items in school, e.g. alcohol, vapes, tobacco
- Child on Child Abuse
- Serious cases of examples given at Level 5

### Example Consequence

External Rustication, exclusion.

### Communication Home

Parent meeting with Head of School and/or Master.

### Who

Head / The Master.

---

## Important Guidance

This table offers guidance to support consistent responses. Each situation may require a different approach depending on context and individual circumstances. Staff should use professional judgement to ensure fair and appropriate outcomes.
