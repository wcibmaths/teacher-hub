Key Stage 3 · Years 7 to 9

Curriculum

Students follow the Pearson Edexcel iLowerSecondary Mathematics curriculum, a UK-based programme designed for international learners aged 11 to 14. It builds a secure foundation across number and algebra, geometry and measures, and statistics and probability.

Students are set into four bands, Ma1 to Ma4. From Year 8 the Ma1 band follows an accelerated scheme and sits a separate accelerated assessment, preparing for early entry to KS4 content.

Teaching approach

  • Structured and sequential delivery. Lessons follow a mapped progression to ensure full coverage and coherent development of core skills.
  • Mastery and depth. Key concepts are revisited through retrieval practice and consolidation before acceleration.
  • Exploration and curiosity. Investigations, open-ended challenges and rich tasks develop critical thinking and mathematical creativity.
  • Real-world connections. Interdisciplinary projects and contextual problems show the relevance of mathematics in science, economics and everyday life.
  • Technology integration. Sparx Maths provides adaptive prep and data-driven insight to personalise support.
  • Inclusive practice. Differentiated instruction ensures all learners are appropriately challenged and supported.

Key Stage 4 · Years 10 to 11

Curriculum

All students follow the Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (Higher Tier) in a linear format, with examinations at the end of Year 11.

  • The core pathway prepares students solely for Mathematics A (Higher Tier).
  • The accelerated pathway, taken by the Ma1 band, combines Mathematics A with the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE in Further Pure Mathematics (4PM1), offering further challenge and depth and providing a direct route into International A Level Further Mathematics.
Further Pure Mathematics replaces Additional Mathematics

From 2026/27 the accelerated pathway uses Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Further Pure Mathematics (4PM1) in place of Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606). This keeps both KS4 qualifications with a single awarding body, aligns the grading scale at 9 to 1 across the pathway, and maps more directly onto International A Level Further Mathematics.

Teaching approach

  • Rigorous and aspirational instruction. High expectations through structured, content-rich lessons focused on deep understanding and fluency.
  • Tailored pathways. Students on the accelerated route receive differentiated content and pacing to manage dual qualifications.
  • Data-informed personalisation. Diagnostic assessment and ongoing tracking support responsive teaching and targeted intervention or extension.
  • Assessment literacy and exam preparation. Regular past-paper practice, mock exams and explicit instruction in exam technique.
  • Enrichment. Students are encouraged to join the Mathematics Squad and take part in UKMT and other competitions.
  • Blended learning. Sparx, Dr Frost, Desmos and interactive resources support independent practice and revision.

Key Stage 5 · Years 12 to 13

Sixth Form students follow the Pearson Edexcel International A Level specifications for Mathematics and Further Mathematics. The curriculum is modular and internationally recognised, offering flexibility, depth and a clear pathway toward university study in mathematics, engineering, economics and related fields. Students sit external examinations at the end of each academic year.

A Level Mathematics · six modules

YearPureApplied
Year 12P1, P2S1
Year 13P3, P4D1 or M1

Four Pure modules and two Applied modules across two years. Year 12 covers the foundational pure content and introductory statistics. In Year 13 students complete the remaining pure modules and take either Decision or Mechanics, depending on interest and post-secondary pathway. Three modules are examined per session.

A Level Further Mathematics · twelve modules

YearPureApplied
Year 12P1, P2, P3, P4S1, M1
Year 13FP1, FP2D1, M2, S2, S3

Further Mathematics is an extension qualification for highly motivated students who wish to explore mathematics in greater depth. Twelve modules are taken, six in each year. Year 12 establishes the pure core alongside introductory statistics and mechanics. Year 13 covers advanced pure content in FP1 and FP2 together with the advanced applied units D1, M2, S2 and S3, allowing students to tailor their learning towards engineering, physics, economics or computer science.

Some units are taught jointly with the A Level Mathematics groups. Y12 Further Mathematics students join the Y12 Maths S1 lesson, and Y13 Further Mathematics students join the Y13 Maths D1 lesson.

Teaching approach at Key Stage 5

  • Advanced curriculum delivery. Depth of understanding across all modules with emphasis on rigour, logical structure and conceptual progression.
  • University and career preparation. Focused exposure to proof, modelling and problem solving, with tailored preparation for MAT, STEP and TMUA.
  • Real-world application. Lessons connect theory to physics, economics, engineering and data science.
  • Growth mindset and resilience. Students are encouraged to persist with multi-step and unfamiliar problems, supported by regular formative feedback.
  • Stretch and mentorship. Further Mathematics students and those pursuing olympiads benefit from optional workshops, seminars and peer mentoring.
  • Independent learning and collaboration. Guided independent study, group investigation and collaborative problem solving build academic maturity.
