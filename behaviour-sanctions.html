Senior School Behaviour and Sanctions
A practical reference for logging behaviour, choosing the appropriate response and communicating with students, tutors, Heads of Year and parents.

1 · Behaviour logging process

One-off

One-off low-level behaviour

  1. Log as a College Concern on iSAMS.
  2. Speak to the student.
Repeated

Repeated low-level behaviour

Examples include behaviour in your subject, behaviour across multiple subjects, lateness and organisation.

Within one subject

Repeated behaviour in your subject

  1. Discuss the proposed Keep In with HoD/HoF.
  2. Record on iSAMS as Level 3, Lunch Time Keep In.
  3. HoY confirms the Keep In date and time by email.
  4. Once confirmed, the staff member giving the Keep In informs the student.
  5. The staff member giving the Keep In emails the parents, copying in the Tutor and HoY.
Across subjects

Repeated behaviour across multiple subjects, lateness or organisation

  1. Tutor can put the student on a Report Card.
  2. Tutor informs the student.
  3. Tutor emails parents, copying in HoY.
Higher-level concern

Lunch Time Keep In

For a one-off high-level behaviour concern where a Lunch Time Keep In is appropriate:

  1. Discuss the proposed Keep In with HoD/HoF.
  2. Record on iSAMS as Level 3, Lunch Time Keep In.
  3. HoY confirms the Keep In date and time by email.
  4. Once confirmed, the staff member giving the Keep In informs the student.
  5. The staff member giving the Keep In emails the parents, copying in the Tutor and HoY.
Higher-level concern

After School Keep In

For a one-off high-level behaviour concern where an After School Keep In is appropriate:

  1. Inform the HoY of the incident.
  2. HoY investigates and speaks with the student.
  3. HoY logs the incident on iSAMS.
  4. HoY informs parents.
  5. HoY issues the Keep In.
Recording

Logging on iSAMS

Use Reward & Conduct Manager.

  • Select the appropriate category.
  • Give a description of what happened.
  • The notification goes to the Tutor, HoY, AHP and DHP.
Follow-up

Report Card and Keep In records

Report Card Template for Tutors: The Report Card Template is also available in the Tutoring & Houses Team.

Keep In Spreadsheet: Managed by HoY/AHP/DHP.

Links to be added when available

The original policy refers to a separate Report Card Template link and Keep In List link. Add the school links here when available.
