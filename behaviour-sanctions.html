  • Repeated Level 3 sanctions

  • Repeated unkindness or high-level, one-off instances of poor behaviour
  • Aggression towards others / physical contact, e.g. pushing / hitting
 After School Keep In, 1 hour. Email to parents.

Parents invited in for a meeting as required.		 Head of Year / Assistant Head.
5 Level 5
  • Repeated Level 4 sanctions
  • Cheating in internal exams, e.g. Mock Exams
  • Bullying-type behaviour
  • Serious physical contact, e.g. hitting
 Internal Rustication, exclusion. Parent meeting to include Deputy Head. Deputy Head / Head.
6 Level 6
  • Multiple instances of Level 4-5 sanctions
  • Bullying
  • Serious physical contact, e.g. hitting
  • Cheating in external exams
  • Possession or use of prohibited items in school, e.g. alcohol, vapes, tobacco
  • Child on Child Abuse
  • Serious cases of examples given at Level 5
 External Rustication, exclusion. Parent meeting with Head of School and/or Master. Head / The Master.
Important guidance

This table offers guidance to support consistent responses. Each situation may require a different approach depending on context and individual circumstances. Staff should use professional judgement to ensure fair and appropriate outcomes.
