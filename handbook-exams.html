Entry structure, paper formats and internal preparation for Key Stage 4 and Key Stage 5. All qualifications are Pearson Edexcel, so entries, materials and grade boundaries come from a single awarding body.

Internal preparation

  • Assessments use past-paper questions aligned to the exam structure.
  • Teachers input CAGs using the 9 to 1 scale for both 4MA1 and 4PM1.
  • Mock performance informs internal tracking and target review.
  • Revision strategies include targeted prep-platform revision homework, error analysis and re-teaching during DIRT, and exam skills sessions in Terms 2 and 3.

Internal preparation and responsibilities

  • Assessments follow the Edexcel modular structure using past papers and official grade boundaries.
  • CAGs are reported using the A* to E A Level scale.
  • Department moderation ensures consistency of marking and standard setting.
  • Students receive modular feedback in Grade, Action, Target format.
  • Revision support includes teacher-led seminars, practice papers and individual action plans based on mock results.

Ownership of A Level exam questions by module is listed on the Teaching Allocations page.

Mock examinations

Mock windows are confirmed each term in the department calendar and published on the Department Hub. Mocks are sat under exam conditions, marked against official mark schemes, and moderated before results are released.

  • Papers are set by the relevant assessment lead for that year group or module.
  • Grade boundaries are agreed in advance across the department.
  • Results feed directly into CAG judgements and target review at the next data drop.
