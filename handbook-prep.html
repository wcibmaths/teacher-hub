Prep
Prep is a critical extension of classroom learning, designed to reinforce key concepts, promote independent practice and provide regular retrieval and fluency building.

Platforms

YearsPlatformLead
Y7 to Y9Sparx MathsHPA
Y10 to Y13Dr Frost MathsADE
1

Setup and alignment

Prep is assigned weekly through the department dashboard. Tasks are generated in retrospect of what was taught the previous week so that prep consolidates recently learned content. Teachers must keep schemes of work up to date on the platform to maintain alignment between teaching and prep.

2

Adaptive personalisation

Sparx adjusts task difficulty to each student's current level, varies the number of questions according to confidence and past performance, and promotes mastery through repetition and spaced retrieval. Prep is therefore differentiated by need even within a set.

3

Bookwork

Students complete prep in their maths exercise books showing all working clearly. Bookwork is routinely checked in class. Missing bookwork gets a clear reminder and a follow-up action. Bookwork goals may be set for students who consistently underperform in this area.

4

Revision and end of term

In the final weeks of each term prep shifts to revision of prior topics using targeted revision tasks or teacher-selected mixed-topic sets. This supports long-term retention ahead of summative assessments and mocks. Paper-based revision packs may supplement for students needing extra practice.

High-impact practice

  • Review prep data regularly to monitor accuracy, completion and effort.
  • Use prep insights to inform intervention such as drop-in support or reteaching key topics.
  • Celebrate consistency and improvement to maintain motivation.
  • Encourage students to reflect on difficult prep questions in class or during feedback loops.

Monitoring and accountability

Completion of prep is an academic expectation for all students. Consistent non-completion is followed up systematically.

  • Teachers monitor completion data weekly via the department dashboard.
  • Repeated missed prep triggers a conversation about learning habits and may lead to targeted intervention or academic support.
  • Persistent non-compliance may lead to a student being placed on Academic Monitoring Report via House or SLT as appropriate.
