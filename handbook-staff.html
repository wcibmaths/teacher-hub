Staff & Roles
The department is led by a collaborative team, each member holding specific responsibility for curriculum, assessment, digital platforms or enrichment. Staff are identified by initials throughout departmental documentation.
Class-by-class detail

This page covers responsibilities. For who teaches which set, hours per class and the full assessment lead tables, see the Teaching Allocations page.

Responsibilities

SSW
Head of DepartmentKS5 Curriculum CoordinatorAssessment Lead · Y9
  • Leads the strategic direction of the department: curriculum planning, assessment design, quality of teaching, staff development and student progress.
  • As KS5 Curriculum Coordinator, oversees planning, sequencing and standards for A Level Mathematics and Further Mathematics, ensuring modular progression aligns with Pearson Edexcel specifications and that Y12 and Y13 are prepared for external examination.
  • Assessment lead for Year 9 (whole year group) and for the Y11 Ma1 further path.
  • Writes and moderates A Level questions for Y12 Maths and FM Statistics S1 and Y13 FM Statistics S2 and S3.
ADE
KS4 Curriculum CoordinatorAssessment Lead · Y7Dr Frost Lead
  • As KS4 Curriculum Coordinator, responsible for planning and quality assurance of the IGCSE Mathematics A and Further Pure Mathematics curriculum, ensuring alignment with Pearson Edexcel specifications.
  • Assessment lead for Year 7 (whole year group) and for the Y8 Ma1 accelerated path.
  • Writes and moderates A Level questions for Y13 Maths P3 and P4.
  • Leads Dr Frost Maths across Y10 to Y13: monitors task-setting consistency and alignment to the scheme of work.
FTA
KS3 Curriculum CoordinatorAssessment Lead · Y11Transition Coordinator
  • Leads curriculum planning and evaluation for Years 7 to 9, ensuring alignment with the Pearson Edexcel iLowerSecondary programme and smooth progression into KS4.
  • Assessment lead for Year 11 (whole year group) and for the Y9 Ma1 accelerated path.
  • Writes and moderates A Level questions for Y12 Maths P1 and P2.
  • Transition coordinator for Year 6 into Year 7: liaison with feeder primaries, baseline assessments and Y7 induction.
MKM
Competitions CoordinatorA Level FM Pure
  • Full ownership of competitions: entries and registrations, deadlines and calendar, UKMT and external body liaison, results tracking, certificates and awards administration.
  • Student selection and communication, parent notifications, problem-solving training sessions, maths club, and celebrating and displaying results.
  • Writes and moderates A Level questions for Y12 Further Maths Pure P1 to P4.
  • Organises Mathematics Squad activities and supports students with advanced problem solving.
HPA
Assessment Lead · Y8 & Y10Sparx LeadDisplay & Enrichment
  • Assessment lead for Year 8 and Year 10 (whole year groups).
  • Writes and moderates A Level questions for Y13 FM Further Pure FP1 and FP2.
  • Leads Sparx Maths across Y7 to Y9: monitors weekly assignment consistency and follows up non-setters.
  • Leads display and enrichment boards, including corridor displays, puzzle of the week and celebrating competition results.
XLN
KS5 onlyDeputy Head
  • Teaches A Level Mathematics and Further Mathematics applied modules to Years 12 and 13.
  • Currently timetabled for Y12 FM M1, Y13 Maths D1 and Y13 FM M2.
  • Supports KS5 assessment marking and contributes to Sixth Form exam preparation.
